The first Trial & Error Symposium brought together researchers, editors, students and science communicators at the University Museum Utrecht.

- Keynote by Dr. Noémie Aubert Bonn: *The Problem of Positive Publication Bias and How to Bring About Cultural Change in Science*
- Dr. Lucie White: *Communicating Uncertainty*
- Art made through trial and error by students of SintLucas, and an exhibition on historical cases of trial and error in science with the Museum of Failure
